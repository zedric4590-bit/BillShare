Appliance usage tracker

List the appliances each tenant uses. Enter the appliance's rated power from its label or manual, then estimate how many hours per day it runs and how many days per month it is used.

How to read the label: Most appliance labels show power in watts (W), not kWh. Estimated energy use = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours used × days used. For example, a 100 W device used for 5 hours a day over 30 days uses about 15 kWh.
